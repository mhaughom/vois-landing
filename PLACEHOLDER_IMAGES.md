## Placeholder Tips
Until you create the actual images, you can:
1. Duplicate existing images and rename them to test the carousel
2. Use screenshot tools to capture different views from your app
3. Create mockups in Figma/Sketch showing different content states

## Testing
The carousel will auto-rotate through all views every 4 seconds. You can also:
- Click the left/right arrows next to "Calendar" to manually navigate
- Clicking arrows pauses auto-scroll
