# Video Animation Prompts

For each icon, generate a 2-3 second looping animation. White background, same flat illustrative style as the static icons. The icon should be centered, filling most of the frame. No text or labels. Smooth motion, subtle and elegant — not over-the-top. Export as WebM or MP4. Square aspect ratio.

---

## Communication & Support

**Email:** A blue open envelope sits centered. The letter inside slowly rises up revealing the @ symbol, pauses briefly, then slides back down. The envelope flap gently lifts as the letter rises. Smooth loop.

**Messenger:** Two overlapping purple chat bubbles. Three dots inside the front bubble animate in sequence (typing indicator — dots bounce up one by one, left to right, then repeat). Subtle and smooth.

**Phone:** A green phone handset gently rocks/tilts side to side as if ringing. Signal wave arcs pulse outward from the earpiece one after another, fading as they expand. Smooth loop.

**Tickets:** An amber/orange ticket stub gently rotates slightly back and forth (like a wiggle). A subtle sparkle/shine sweeps across the star on the ticket. Smooth loop.

**Client Portal:** A sky blue doorway/portal. A person silhouette walks through the door from left to right, the door swings open as they approach and closes gently behind them. Smooth loop.

**Scheduling Links:** A teal chain link icon. The two links gently pull apart slightly then snap back together. A small clock on one link has its hands spinning. Smooth loop.
